Both glasses hold the same 68‑year‑old. At left, no intervention: the sand pours at full flow and the epigenome reads 74 — six years of age acceleration. At right, comprehensive intervention has tipped the glass. The sand runs slower and biological age reads 67. The birth date cannot be changed; the trajectory of cardiovascular disease can be slowed — and the epigenetic clock is where that slowing becomes measurable.

The coral hand of the argument — chronological age — never moves off 68.
Methylation marks on the outer strand open as each intervention is applied.
Sand re‑levels under gravity as the glass tips; the stream at the neck thins to a trickle.
The tip stops short of inversion. Flow slows — it never stops, and elapsed time is not recovered.

*Comprehensive intervention: Mediterranean diet, regular physical activity, improved sleep quality, social participation, and risk‑factor pharmacotherapy. Values shown are illustrative and are described in full in the article.
